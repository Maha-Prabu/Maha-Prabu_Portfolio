Who I Am

About Me

I'm Mahaprabu K, a motivated IT enthusiast with roots in mechanical engineering and real-world manufacturing experience. My journey started on the production floor at Daimler India, where I learned discipline, process thinking, and zero-defect mindset.

After years of hands-on work in assembly operations, site supervision, and quality inspection, I realized my true calling lay in technology. I started teaching myself Python, diving deep into networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, DNS, subnetting), and exploring the world of cybersecurity.

"The discipline I built on the factory floor — zero defects, process rigor, constant improvement — is exactly what I bring to IT and cybersecurity."

My mechanical background gave me something many IT freshers don't have: a deep understanding of how complex systems work, how failures cascade, and how to think methodically under pressure. Now I'm channeling that into networking, automation, and security.
